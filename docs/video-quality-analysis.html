AI video quality analysis

Reel gives an agent the picture and the corresponding sound on one timeline. It can replay the evidence behind a suspected editing problem.

Frozen frames

In the generated demo, the picture holds from 38 to 41 seconds while the audio continues. Set the viewing cutoff to at least 42 seconds, advance through the interval, and ask the agent to identify and replay the hold.

observe(start_s=38, end_s=41)

See the actual frame sequence and demo. A still shot may be intentional, so the model should inspect the scene before calling it a fault.

Repeated sounds and subtitle timing

Compare two suspected audio repeats using their original excerpts. Inspect when an on-screen subtitle appears relative to the available speech evidence. Sound recognition and transcription can miss events, so keep the time ranges and evidence in the review.
